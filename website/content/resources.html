Check the audit evidence

ClamAV reported zero detections for the 0.3.2 source ZIP and the archive built by the release script. Use Google Play → Choose APKs... → Check scan records to compare local files with six scanned APKs by SHA-256. One legacy check timed out on Play services and Play Store.

The installer verifies Google’s signature and the installed bytes. Tests used a simulated tablet. Physical-tablet installation and sign-in remain unverified. Audit, checksums and test results · Security and local state

Keep your undo files

Keep this folder on your computer to restore ad, package and settings changes.

  • Windows uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\open-fire-toolkit, with the home folder as fallback.
  • Linux uses $XDG_STATE_HOME/open-fire-toolkit or ~/.local/state/open-fire-toolkit.
