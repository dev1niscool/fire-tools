Remove Special Offers through Amazon

  1. Open Lock-screen ads → Open Amazon device settings or visit Manage Your Devices. USB is optional.
  2. Select your Fire tablet. Choose Special Offers → Remove offers to see its one-time price, if available.
  3. Choose End Offers and Pay the Fee to accept Amazon’s charge. Amazon’s removal instructions.

What to expect on Fire OS 8

Amazon listed 8.3.3.8 for Fire HD 10 (13th generation) on November 27, 2026. Fire Toolbox v46 treats Fire OS 8 builds above 8.3.3.7 as restricted. Firmware may block package changes, ignore the setting or reset it. Check Settings → Device Options → System Updates. Amazon’s update list · v46 firmware comparison.

Lock and wake the tablet to check actual ad visibility. The project tested state changes in simulation. Ad suppression and persistence after updates remain unverified on hardware. Check other firmware versions.

Optional on-tablet helper

On API 24+, the helper reapplies the setting with a visible notification. Verify the local build and signer before granting secure-settings permission. Use Stop before desktop undo or Restore to recover the saved value before clearing data or uninstalling. Build, install and restore the helper.
