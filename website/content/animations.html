Shorten animations on your tablet

At 0.5×, a transition normally lasting half a second takes about a quarter second. Opening a menu can feel quicker. App loading still depends on the app and hardware. You can do this entirely on the tablet with USB debugging off.

  1. Open Settings → Device Options → Developer Options. If the menu is hidden, open About Fire Tablet, tap Serial Number seven times, then return to Device Options. Turn on the Developer Options switch if shown. Amazon’s menu instructions.
  2. Find Window animation scale, Transition animation scale and Animator duration scale. Look under Drawing, or scroll through Developer Options for those names.
  3. Note each current value, then tap each setting and choose 0.5×. Go Home and reopen Settings to try the change. Android animation controls · Animator duration.

Restore your original speed

Return to the same three settings and put each back to the value you recorded. Choose 1× on all three for standard timing.

Change all three with Fire Tools

With your tablet connected and selected, open Tools → Display and sounds → Animation speed. Set Animation scale to 0.5, choose Review, then Confirm and run. Repeat with 1 for standard timing. Connect your tablet over USB.
